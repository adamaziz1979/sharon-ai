Stage 1: One-page Executive Briefing

Company in one paragraph

Observed Fact Sharon AI (SharonAI Holdings Inc., Nasdaq: SHAZ) is an Australian "neocloud". It rents NVIDIA GPU capacity to AI labs, AI platforms and enterprises as cloud compute, mostly on five-year take-or-pay contracts, from NEXTDC and Equinix data centres in Melbourne and Sydney, with New Zealand next. It began as a small distributed-storage and HPC business (DSS, 2021), listed in the US through a SPAC merger in December 2025 and moved to Nasdaq in February 2026. By 6 Aug 2026 it reported about US$8.8bn of total contract value, including a US$4.9bn six-year NVIDIA collaboration, and 212 MW of secured capacity. Revenue is still tiny (US$1.57m in CY25). Strong Inference The next 18 months are about turning that capacity and more than 64,000 GPUs (expected by mid-2027) into billed revenue.

Why the Role Exists Now

Strong Inference Working Hypotheses H1 to H3
In about nine months Sharon AI went from 30 people and US$1.57m of revenue to about US$8.8bn of TCV, 212 MW secured and US$2.32bn of assets. Now customers, contracts, GPUs, power, SLAs and usage must join up for:
  • the COO and CFO, who are both new;
  • EY's first audit and the listed-company controls that follow a 2025 material weakness;
  • lenders in a planned series of GPU-backed facilities, who need data per GPU pool.
The company wants this built by one hands-on leader using agents (Claude named in the ad), not by a large data team. Hiring backs this up: 38 open roles in Australia (27 in Sydney) against 30 full-time staff, plus a new Sydney Data & AI team (see Recruitment Activity).

2. How the Company Became What It Is Today

2021 to 2024: Roll-up
Small Australian storage and compute businesses (DSS and AAM, both from 2021) were brought under a Delaware holding company formed in Feb 2024. NVIDIA Cloud Partner status (Dec 2024) and a 1,000-plus GPU supercluster at NEXTDC M3 gave it credibility.
2025: Listing route
It chose a SPAC (Roth CH: agreement Jan 2025, closed 17 Dec 2025) for US market access, launched SharonAI Cloud (Feb 2025) and hired CTO Daniel Mons (May 2025).
Jan to Feb 2026: Focus
Co-founder James Manning became CEO. The Texas gas-powered data-centre joint venture was sold for US$74m, putting the focus on Australia and APAC "sovereign AI". The shares moved to Nasdaq with a US$125m offering at US$30.
Apr to Aug 2026: Scale
Large five-year contracts and the NVIDIA collaboration took TCV to about US$8.8bn. Funding came from convertible notes (US$103m Dec 2025, US$350m Apr/May 2026, US$700m Jun 2026), equity (US$125m Feb, about US$900m Jun) and, from Oct, GPU-backed debt (US$356m).
Aug to Oct 2026: Institutionalise
An experienced CFO and COO arrived, co-founder Andrew Leece moved to strategic partnerships, and EY was appointed. Strong Inference It is now a capital-heavy operator with a small team, a very large contract book and little billed revenue yet.

Stage 3: Top 5 Experiences to Lead With

The COO and executives will probably want proof of three things: you can personally build with agents and Claude, you can make data trusted and auditable, and you can model a large operational fleet and turn it into commercial decisions. Each of these five covers a different part of that, and each answers part of the business problem.

1

KPI Creator: a five-agent team built in Claude Code (personal build, 2026)

Why it matters here: The ad asks for "significant practical experience with LLMs, agents, tool use" and nice-to-have "experience with Anthropic Claude". It answers H3 (agent-first company) directly.
STurning a business problem into a KPI dashboard takes many hand-offs: requirements, KPI logic, charts and the build. TBuild a repeatable, human-in-the-loop agent team that carries a KPI from problem to dashboard. ADesigned five agents in Claude Code, each with one job: an orchestrator and approval gate (Ovy), business requirements (Gary), KPI development (Kewpie), data visualisation (Visalli) and dashboard creation (Jennifer). Added stage-by-stage approval, test cases and token-use reporting. RRuns end to end on test projects (ClearCompare, Kestrel Medical), with an agent simulation replaying each run. One of 23 AI builds (20 in Claude Code, about 40 hours of build time).
"The closest thing to what you're describing is a five-agent team I built in Claude Code, where each agent owns one stage and a human approves every handover."
Likely question: "Tell us about an agent system you've actually built." · Source: Portfolio site (KPI Creator, AI builds); Master Resume (Building with Claude API certification). Be precise This is a personal build, not a Cisco production system. Say so, then explain the controls you built in.
2

AI assistant on trusted data for support-case spikes (Cisco, Data Solutions & Insights)

Why it matters here: The ad asks you to "make trusted enterprise data safely accessible to people, applications and AI agents" and to automate analytical work.
SAfter layoffs, the global IT support function had fewer agents and handled case spikes reactively; executives heard about issues before service owners did. TAlert the right teams early and give them instant, reliable insight without switching apps. ABuilt two-tier tripwire alerts (1.8 and 2 standard deviations over six months of case trends). Led a GPT-based assistant in Webex chat, with its knowledge base on custom SQL views, prompt patterns (cognitive verifier, question refinement, few-shot) and a QA process for accuracy. RCase-spike analysis went from 4 days to near-instant insight, with fewer executive escalations and surprises.
"I've learned that an AI assistant is only as good as the data under it, so I built this one on verified SQL views, not raw tickets."
Likely question: "How do you make enterprise data safe and useful for AI?" · Source: Master Resume (Data Solutions & Insights Leader); STAR "AI Assistant"; portfolio case study 2. Figure conflict The resume says 4 days; STAR and the portfolio say half a day. Confirm which before the interview.
3

Resilient data foundations: lineage, release cycles and secure sharing (Cisco, Snowflake and DBT)

Why it matters here: The ad asks for "security, ownership, quality, lineage, provenance, retention and lifecycle controls" and "pragmatic controls appropriate to a listed-company environment". It answers H2 (audit-ready source of truth).
SAfter moving to Snowflake, changes broke dashboards, errors were hard to trace, and external access risked crashing the warehouse for 200+ stakeholders. TShape a scalable, maintainable architecture with sensible tool choices. AAdded a DBT transformation and governance layer with lineage and DBT Docs (replacing Alation), GitHub release cycles with peer review and YAML tests, regular data purges, and secure AWS S3 data sharing instead of direct warehouse access. RWarehouse volume down 42% (355 to 205 tables and views; 517.2M stagnant rows removed). Disruptions went from 1 to 2 a quarter to zero, protecting reporting for 200+ stakeholders.
"When I hear 'auditable without bureaucracy', I think of how we added lineage and release cycles to our Snowflake stack without slowing the team down."
Likely question: "How would you set up data controls for a listed company?" · Source: Master Resume (Data Solutions & Insights Leader); STAR "Building resilient data foundations"; portfolio case study 7.
4

One trusted set of KPIs across the business (Cisco, Data Solutions & Insights)

Why it matters here: The ad asks for "coherent models" of the business; the business problem is "one trusted model". It answers H1 (separate tools, separate truths).
STeams saw different numbers for the same KPI (1 to 5% gaps), causing 3 to 5 escalations a month. TGet to zero inconsistency and restore confidence in the numbers. AMoved logic out of Power BI into shared SQL single-source views, added plain-English catalogues, built master dashboards per business area, aligned refresh timings and set tripwire alerts for variances over 2%. R27 KPIs went from 62% to 100% consistency. Dashboards cut from 70+ to 46. Escalations from 3 to 5 a month to zero. Workspace health from 60% to 93%.
"The first thing I usually fix is whether people trust the number. At Cisco, the same KPI showed different values depending on which dashboard you opened."
Likely question: "How would you create one source of truth across Sales, Finance and Operations?" · Source: Master Resume (Data Solutions & Insights Leader); STAR "Trusted metrics and KPI Consistency"; portfolio case study 1.
5

Fleet telemetry and anomaly detection for laptop refresh (Cisco, about 150,000 devices)

Why it matters here: The ad asks for "digital-twin and related modelling approaches" and "judgement about when to build, buy, integrate, automate". Modelling a large device fleet from telemetry is the closest match to modelling a GPU fleet.
SLaptops were refreshed by age, not need, across a fleet of about 150,000 laptop and mobile devices, so budget and supply didn't match user needs. TRefresh based on evidence from device telemetry. ACompared supervised ML, unsupervised ML, GenAI and statistical methods on build effort, upkeep and reliability. Chose unsupervised anomaly detection. Defined the telemetry strategy (CPU, RAM, memory pressure, disk via OpenTelemetry) and built persona clusters from app usage. RPrioritised early refresh for underperforming devices, cut over-provisioning risk, improved stock forecasting and supported millions in potential global asset savings.
"Your GPU fleet is a bigger version of a problem I've worked on: using telemetry to decide what each asset should do next."
